# ClearStream™ 2.0: Meta ad copy, ads 1–10

This is the text that runs in Ads Manager alongside each creative. Every ad gets 3 headline options and primary text in three lengths.

**Rules applied to every line**
- Never says or implies that the filter softens water.
- Only names what it reduces: chlorine, rust, sediment and metals.
- No "99%", no chloramine, no skin-condition claims, no "50% off" and no countdowns.
- Guarantee wording follows the PDP exactly: 60 days, full refund by email, nothing to ship back.
- No em dashes.
- The price ($59.99) appears only in the back half of the long versions.
- Quotes are copied word for word from the reviews shown on the PDP. **Confirm these are real customer reviews before running any quote copy.** The store has no review app, so I can't verify them.
- The filter-life claim is left out on purpose, because the PDP says both 3 months and 6 months.

**CTA button:** Shop Now for all ads, except ad 2 and ad 7, which use Learn More.
