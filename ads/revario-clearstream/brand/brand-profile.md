# Revario — Brand Profile

Scraped from revario.co on 2026-09-24 (theme CSS, product JSON, logo file, product imagery).

## Identity
- **Store:** Revario (revario.co), Shopify, Dawn-family theme
- **Logo:** `assets/revario-logo.png`. Lowercase geometric sans wordmark `revario.` with a period, in #1E1E1E on transparent
- **Product mark:** "Clear**Stream**™ 2.0". "Clear" is black and "Stream" is blue, as used on every product image

## Typography
| Role | Font | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | Montserrat | 700 |
| Body | Montserrat | 400 / 700 |

Free on Google Fonts. The ad overlays should use Montserrat ExtraBold/Bold for headlines and Montserrat Medium for body.
(The product images also use a condensed display face, like Bebas or Oswald, and a Didone-style serif. Those faces came from the image generator and are not the brand's, so don't treat them as brand fonts.)

## Voice (from the PDP's own copy)
Plain, dry, honest, second-person. For example: "Nobody treats it to be pleasant to stand under." "Two things worth being straight about." "If you can change a lightbulb, you can do this." The strongest copy on the site is the FAQ, which says outright that the filter is *not* a softener. Ads should use this voice, not the generic "A Healthier You" voice of the product images.

## Product facts (true as stated on the PDP)
- ClearStream™ 2.0, a 20-stage inline shower filter. It goes between the shower arm and your existing head, uses standard ½" NPT, and needs no tools. Install takes about 60 seconds.
- Finishes: Black, Brushed Nickel, Oil Rubbed Bronze, Chrome
- Price: $59.99 (compare-at $119.99, "Save 50%", "Sale Ends Today")
- Media: sediment → KDF-55 → mineral/alkaline → final sediment
- 60-day guarantee, refund with no return required. 1-yr warranty (2-yr with 2+ filters). Free shipping.
- Review count shown: 1,429. Also claims "4,000+ Happy Customers".
