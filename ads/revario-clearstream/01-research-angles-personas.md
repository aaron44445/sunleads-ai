## 1. Claim check (read before writing copy)

| Claim on site | What's true | Ad rule |
|---|---|---|
| "For hard water" (product URL/title) | Shower filters do **not** soften water. Independent testing of a leading competitor found 0% hardness reduction. Revario's own FAQ says the same. | Never say "softens" or "removes hardness". Say "for homes with hard water" and name what it *does* remove. |
| "KDF-55 cuts chlorine by up to 99%" | KDF hits roughly 90–99% on day 1 under lab conditions. In real hot showers it degrades: one real-world test of a KDF filter measured 54%, and the category drops sharply by week 4–8. | Keep "99%" out of ads. "Reduces chlorine" is safe. |
| "Filters chloramine" | KDF/sediment media do little against chloramine. Vitamin C media is what handles it. | Leave chloramine out of ads. Also worth fixing on the PDP. |
| Replace every **3 months** (description) vs **~6 months** (FAQ) | The PDP contradicts itself | Pick one before running cost-per-month copy |
| "1,429 Reviews" vs "4,000+ Happy Customers" | Two different numbers | Use only the review count, and only if it's real |
| $119.99 → $59.99 "Sale Ends Today" | A permanent compare-at price plus a countdown is an FTC/Meta risk | Keep "50% off" out of the image |
| **Product images 03–06** (hair/skin/scalp before/afters) | Filenames are `ChatGPT_Image_…`. These are AI-generated faces and results. | **Do not use in ads.** They are fabricated result claims, which lead to ad rejection and chargebacks. |
| "Dermatologist Recommended" (img 06), "Lab tested" (img 07) | No source anywhere on the page | Don't reuse unless there's a real document behind it |

**Real product photo needed.** All 8 store images, including the hero, look AI-rendered. Grounded-edit ads need a photo of the actual unit that ships (a phone shot on a white counter is fine), ideally one per finish, plus **a real photo of a used cartridge after 30 days**. That one photo powers the strongest angle below.

## 2. What the customer says

(Reddit blocks automated fetch, so this comes from review sites, test write-ups and the PDP. For more verbatims, pull Reddit manually from r/HaircareScience, r/curlyhair, r/Renters, r/phoenix and r/vegas.)

Language people reach for unprompted:
- "my hair **turned to straw**" / "hair like **dry straw**" (Arizona, SoCal, Texas, Vegas)
- "scalp **itched every morning**"
- "my hair looks and feels nicer **on vacation** in other cities, I guess it's the water"
- color-treated hair "**immediately went off a cliff**" after moving
- "**the water feels noticeably better**" / "such a **simple upgrade**" (PDP)
- "**blends right into my shower**" / "doesn't feel bulky" (PDP. The look matters.)
- negatives: "**weaker spray than expected**", "**didn't notice a difference**", "didn't fit", "**waste of money**"

**Objections, in order:** (1) it's a placebo or a scam, (2) it'll kill my water pressure, (3) it won't fit, (4) it looks ugly/bulky, (5) another subscription.
The PDP already answers all five: the no-return refund, the flow-through FAQ, ½" NPT, four finishes, and the visible cartridge.

**Psychological drivers**
- *Attention:* the orange cartridge. It's gross, it's visual, and you can read it at thumbnail size.
- *Curiosity:* "why is my hair better on vacation?"
- *Emotion:* $200 color jobs fading, money wasted on products, "it's all in my head".
- *Connection:* "the city I moved to wrecked my hair" is a shared identity (Phoenix, Vegas, LA, Austin, Denver).

## 3. Competitor landscape (Meta, US, image ads)

The longest runners (see `assets/competitors/_top-running-image-ads.jpg`):
- **Santevia**, 167 days: product in a lush waterfall scene with arrow callouts. "Chlorine removes sebum… restores your glow."
- **Hello Klean**, 153 days: moody macro of a brushed-metal head. "The only shower filter that tracks filter life."
- **Primals**, 138 days: fear/news format, CT scan + "NEW STUDY: … 33% higher bladder risk". Also "People rebuild their whole shower routine and skip the water."
- **Advertorial pages** ("Sarah Miller", "Isabella Morgan"): long first-person stories about doctors dismissing it. Around 191 days for the baby-skin one.

**Recurring territory:** chrome product on marble, before/after hair, fear of toxins, "ritual".
**Gaps nobody is running:**
1. **Honesty as the hook.** No one says what their filter *can't* do. Revario already wrote this copy in its FAQ.
2. **The used cartridge as hero.** Everyone shows a clean new product. Proof lives on a dirty one.
3. **Renters.** It's the perfect product for them (no tools, reversible, keeps the landlord's head), and nobody talks to them.
4. **Relocation identity.** City-specific "your new city did this to your hair".

## 6. Recommended first four (one ad set, four genuinely different ads)

**Ad 1: "30 days of your shower." (A1, TOF)**
★ Before/After · Attention + Emotion
A wet hand holds a used orange-brown cartridge next to a white new one, lit side-on against `#172830` deep slate. Headline top-left in Montserrat ExtraBold white; "Day 1 / Day 30" labels.
Body: "Every shower, this is what your hair and skin were standing under. ClearStream screws on between your arm and your showerhead in 60 seconds. The cartridge is the proof."
**Needs a real used-cartridge photo.** A generated one would be a fabricated result.

**Ad 2: "It was never the shampoo." (A2, TOF)**
ICP Mirror · Connection + Emotion
Top-down on a crammed shower caddy of generic, unbranded half-used bottles. A hand reaches past them to the chrome filter on the arm. Warm, real, slightly messy. `#F3F3F3` tile, headline in the `#121212` negative space.
Body: "Bond repair. Clarifying shampoo. The $40 mask. If your hair went to straw when you moved, it's not your routine. It's the chlorine and metals in your water."

**Ad 3: "This won't soften your water." (A4, MOF)**
Controversial Take + ★ Monochromatic World · Curiosity + Connection
The product alone, Brushed Nickel, in a fully deep-slate `#172830` set, with one hard key light and water droplets. Big white type. Sub: "Here's what it actually does."
Body is lifted straight from the FAQ: "True softening takes a salt-tank system. What ClearStream does is remove the chlorine, sediment and metals that ride along with hard water. For most people that's the part they were actually feeling. 60 days. If you feel no difference, we refund you. Keep the filter."

**Ad 4: "No landlord. No plumber. No tools." (A5, TOF/MOF)**
ICP Mirror · Connection + Attention
POV close-up of two hands threading the filter onto a shower arm in a basic rental bathroom (white builder-grade tile). Negative space above for the three-beat headline in Montserrat Bold, with the third beat in `#16943E`.
Body: "Standard ½" thread. Fits fixed, handheld and rain heads. Unscrews in 60 seconds when you move out."

Human presence is in 3 of 4. Two are ★ formats. No price in any image.
